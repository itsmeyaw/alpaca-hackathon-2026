Model Architecture

One narrow path to an order.

flowchart LR
  MD[Alpaca Market Data API] --> U[Universe] --> M[XGBoost] -->|Signal| R[Risk Governor] --> E[Execution] --> PT[Alpaca Paper Trading API]
  N[Alpaca News API] -. shadow events .-> L[(Ledger)]
  PT -->|Reconcile| E
  M --> L
  R --> L

The XGBoost model can propose a trade. Only the Risk Governor can approve an Alpaca order.

Boosted trees350
Max depth4
Point-in-time inputs16
Prediction horizon4h

The classifier estimates a positive four-hour move. A probability of 52% or higher indicates enough upside edge for a long position. A probability of 48% or lower indicates the corresponding downside edge for a short position. The 48-52% band abstains from weak signals. This narrow symmetric gate permits more paper trading opportunities while deterministic risk controls retain final authority.

Built To Survive

Every trade must earn
permission to exist.

Market gates
Freshness, spread, hours
Bad or stale data resolves to no trade.
Portfolio limits
1% risk per trade
Six positions max, 5% total stop-risk.
Failure containment
Brackets + circuit breakers
Risk halt at 4% daily loss; kill at 12% drawdown.

Auditable by design: immutable decisions, idempotent order claims, and Alpaca reconciliation after every restart.
